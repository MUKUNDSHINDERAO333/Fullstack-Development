Classic Fluffy Pancakes

A quick and easy recipe to make perfectly soft, golden, and delicious homemade pancakes from scratch.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup of all-purpose flour
  • 1 cup of fresh milk
  • 3 large fresh eggs
  • 2 tablespoons of melted butter

Instructions

  1. Whisk the flour and eggs together in a large mixing bowl.
  2. Gradually pour in the milk while continuing to stir until smooth.
  3. Heat a lightly greased skillet or pan over medium heat.
  4. Pour batter onto the skillet, flip when bubbles form, and cook until golden brown.
